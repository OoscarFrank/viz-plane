# Radar enclosure (Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7)

Three parts that print without supports. Parametric source: `radar_enclosure.scad` (OpenSCAD).

| Part | Colour | Size (mm) | Print orientation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `frame.stl` | orange | 202 × 120 × 7 | front face on the bed |
| `shell.stl` | black | 198 × 116 × 24 | back on the bed |
| `stand.stl` | black | 198 × 116 × 109 | shell-side face on the bed, leg up |

Bed of at least 205 × 125 mm. PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill.

`radar_enclosure_bambu.3mf` is a Bambu Studio project with three plates: plate 1 (stand) and plate 3 (shell) on filament 1, plate 2 (frame) on filament 2. `stand_bambu.3mf` holds the stand alone. The stand and the shell carry fast settings (0.28 mm layers, 2 walls, 10% gyroid infill); the frame keeps the printer defaults since it is the visible face. Both files are generated by `build_3mf.py`. `viewer.html` is a 3D viewer of the assembly.

## Hardware

Everything comes from the kit of 340 M3 stainless button-head screws (ISO 7380), 5 to 20 mm.

- 4 × M3 × 20: stand → shell → screen. They go through the stand and the shell into the screen's brass inserts and hold the three parts together. Their heads sit flush in the back of the stand.

The 4 holes near the centre of the shell's back are left over from the first stand and are no longer used; the stand covers them.

## Stand

An easel in one piece: a 5 mm plate covering the whole back of the shell, and a leg reaching back to the table (leg 70 mm wide at the plate, 116 mm on the table, so a screwdriver clears it). It prints without supports with the shell-side face on the bed and the leg rising at about 64°.

## Assembly

1. Route the USB-C cable through the slot in the right side wall and plug it into the board.
2. Drop the screen into the shell, glass facing out.
3. Put the stand against the back of the shell and screw the 4 × M3 × 20 through the stand and the shell into the screen.
4. Push the frame on. Add a dot of glue or double-sided tape if it moves.

## Check on the real board before printing

The dimensions come from the Waveshare drawing and 3D model, not from a measured board.

- Spacing of the 4 M3 holes: 126.2 × 65.65 mm.
- USB-C ports: right edge seen from the front, 51.8 and 66.9 mm from the top of the glass. Pick the port with `usb_port`.
- Depth of the screen's M3 inserts: 2 mm of thread engaged by default (`screw_engage`).
- Tallest component: 18.4 mm behind the glass (`COMP_Z`).

To regenerate the STL files after a change: `openscad -D 'part="shell"' -o shell.stl radar_enclosure.scad` (same for `frame` and `stand`). For the Bambu projects, export the three parts as `.3mf` into a folder the same way, then run `python3 build_3mf.py <folder>` and `python3 build_3mf.py <folder> stand`.
